# Token logos

Token brand images downloaded from the official TxRelay asset service:
`https://static.txrelay.com/static/logo/{SYMBOL}.png`.

These images identify the corresponding assets; trademarks remain with their owners.
1000BONK uses the BONK logo. Unknown assets use a text fallback rather than another token’s logo.

Included symbols: AAVE, APT, ARB, AVAX, BNB, BONK, BTC, DOGE, ENA, ETH, LINK, ONDO, OP, SHIB, SOL, SUI, UNI, USDC, USDT

Additional SVGs: https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons (CC0 1.0).
Symbols: 1INCH, ADA, ANKR, ATOM, BCH, CRV, DOT, ETC, FIL, ICP, LTC, TRX, XLM, XRP

Additional SVGs: https://github.com/0xa3k5/web3icons (MIT).
Symbols: CAKE, CRO, HBAR, ID, MNT, POL, SEI, STRK, TAO, XAUT

Ticker collisions are not substituted: the legacy SKY/Skycoin icon is excluded for the Sky governance market.
